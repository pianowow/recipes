# Farmer's Market Umami Soup

This recipe builds on a sweet base of root vegetables, layering in rich, savory flavors from mushrooms, miso, and spices. The addition of kale makes it a complete and hearty meal.

### Instructions

1.  **Preparation (Mise en Place):**
    *   Press **1 block (14-16 oz) firm or extra-firm tofu** for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water.  Once pressed, cut it into 1-inch cubes.
    *   While the tofu presses, peel and chop the **3 carrots**, **3 parsnips**, **2 medium yellow onions**, and **1 shallot**.  Mince the **5 cloves of garlic**.
    *   Rinse and slice the **1 leek** (white and light green parts) and the **8 oz cremini mushrooms**.  Wash and chop **1 bunch of kale** into 1-inch pieces.
    *   Tie the stems from **1 bunch of flat-leaf parsley** and **1 bunch of dill** together with kitchen twine, tucking **1 bay leaf** inside to create an herb wrap.

2.  **Sauté Aromatics & Mushrooms:** Heat a large stock pot with a drizzle of **olive oil** over medium-high heat.  Add the chopped onions, shallot, and leek.  Season with a pinch of **salt and freshly ground black pepper** to help the vegetables soften.  Sauté until translucent, about 8 minutes, stirring occasionally.  Add the sliced mushrooms, season them lightly with salt, and continue to cook until they have released their liquid and started to brown, about 5-7 minutes.

3.  **Build the Flavor Base:** Stir in **1 tbsp tomato paste** and cook for one minute until it darkens slightly.  Add the minced garlic, **1 tsp smoked paprika**, and **1/2 tsp ground cumin**.  Cook for another minute until fragrant.

4.  **Simmer the Soup:** Add the chopped carrots, parsnips, the herb wrap, and **2 quarts of vegetable stock** to the pot.  Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 20 minutes, or until the carrots and parsnips are tender.  Taste the broth here and add more salt if needed, but be mindful that the miso will add saltiness later.

5.  **Add Tofu and Kale:** Gently stir in the cubed tofu and continue to simmer for 5 minutes.  Add the chopped kale and stir for another 2-3 minutes, until it has wilted into the soup.

6.  **Finish with Miso & Acid:** Remove the pot from the heat and discard the herb wrap.  In a small bowl, whisk **1 tbsp white miso paste** with a ladleful of the warm soup broth until smooth.  This prevents clumping.  Stir the dissolved miso mixture back into the soup.

7.  **Serve:** Stir in **1 tsp red wine vinegar** (or other vinegar/lemon juice).  Do a final tasting and adjust seasoning with more salt and pepper if needed.  Let the soup rest for 5 minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld.
