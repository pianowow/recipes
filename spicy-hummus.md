# Spicy Hummus

Mix *3.5 Tbsp Rumi Harissa spice blend* with *1.5 tsp cayenne pepper* and *1 Tbsp hot water* thoroughly to hydrate the spices and let sit for 10 minutes.

Add *1 Tbsp Olive Oil* and *1.5 tsp tomato paste* and stir to form a thick spreadable paste.  Incorporate this mixture into a *38 oz tub of Basha Original Hommus* from Costco.  Mix thoroughly until evenly distributed.  Let sit 15 minutes.

For slightly spicier and simpler incorporation, use some of the hummus before adding the paste.
