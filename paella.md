### Plant Paella

- Put a genersous **pinch of saffron** into 2 tbsp water.
- Dice **2 large vine ripe tomatoes (230g), 1 red bell pepper (160g), one large yellow onion (260g)**.
- Slice **5 garlic cloves**.
- Reserve 1 cup from a **quart of vegetable broth**.
<!-- -->
- Heat a large skillet with high sides and lid.  
- Dry saute the onion about 8 minutes until translucent.  
- Add garlic.  Stir until fragrant. 
- Add tomatoes, **1.5c brown rice** with saffron and water, **salt and pepper** to taste.  Stir to coat.  
- Add 3c vegetable broth and diced pepper, **1 tbsp paprika**.  Stir to incorporate.  
- Cover and bring the mixture to a boil, simmering for 25-30 minutes.  Ensure steam rolls off the lid back into the pan.  Do not mix the rice again.
<!-- -->
- Drain and rinse a **15 oz can of garbanzo beans** and a **14 oz can of artichoke hearts**.  Cut the ends of 260g asparagus and cut into three equal lengths.  
<!-- -->
- When the liquid has mostly been absorbed by the rice, add garbanzo beans, artichoke hearts, asparagus, and **1 tbsp lemon juice** around the top of the pan.  Pour the reserved cup of broth around the whole pan, jiggling to ensure proper distribution.  Saute an additional 10 minutes uncovered to develop the socarrat, jiggling occasionally.
<!-- -->
- When the liquid has evaporated, check the rice for doneness.  Additional water/broth can be added until it is done 1/4c at a time.  
<!-- -->
- When the rice is done, remove from heat, cover, and rest for 5 minutes.
<!-- -->
- Chop **1/4c flat leaf parsley**
- Slice **avocado**
<!-- -->
- Garnish with flat leaf parsley, avocado slices, **crushed red pepper** and/or **sriracha sauce**.
