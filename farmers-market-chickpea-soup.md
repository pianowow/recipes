# Farmer's Market Chickpea Soup

- Peel and chop **3 carrots, 3 parsnips, 5 cloves of garlic, 1 shallot, 2 medium yellow onions.**
- Rinse and slice **1 leek (white and greens)**
- Heat a large stock pot until it is very hot.  Add onion, shallot, leek, and saute until translucent, about 8 minutes, stirring occasionally.  
- Meanwhile, prepare **1 bunch of flat-leaf parsley, and 1 bunch of dill** wrapped with twine.  
- Add garlic and cook for about a minute until fragrant.
- Add carrots, parsnips, herb wrap, and **2 quarts of vegetable stock**.  Bring to a boil, reduce heat and cover, simmering for 20 minutes or until carrots and parsnips are tender.  Add a teaspon of **white balsamic venegar or outher source of acid** and adjust for seasoning. 
- Drain **2 cans of chickpeas**, and chop **1 bunch of kale** into 1 inch pieces.  
- Add chickpeas and kale and bring back to a boil.  Remove from heat, discard the herb wrap, and let rest for 5 minutes.
