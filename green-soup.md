# Green Soup

- Cut **one head of cauliflower**, and less **broccoli and/or asparagus** into 2 inch pieces

- Dice **one onion** and chop **5 cloves of garlic**

- In a large soup pot, sautee onion until translucent.  Add garlic and stir until fragrant.

- Add cauliflower and broccoli/aparagus and cover with **vegetable broth / seasoned water**.  Bring to a boil on high, then simmer.

- Salt to taste 

- When cauliflower is tender, blend with an immersian blender.  

- Add **coconut milk or cream or other plant milk** if desired.
