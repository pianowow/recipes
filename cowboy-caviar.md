# Cowboy Caviar

1. Dice a **jalapeño, red pepper, orange pepper, and half a red onion** into corn-sized pieces.

2. Drain a **can of corn, can of black eyed peas, a can of black beans, and a can of diced tomatoes**

3. Mix everything together in a large bowl.

4. Add **salt, pepper, olive oil, garlic power, and cilantro** to taste
