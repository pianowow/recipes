# Carrot Cake

- This recipe prepares one 8 inch layer.  Multiply for as many layers as desired. 

- Shred **200g of carrots**.  This should be about one cup packed.  In a mixing bowl, whisk together **1/2 cup apple sauce**, **1/4 cup oil, or extra applesauce**, **2 tsp white or cider vinegar**, **2 tsp pure vanilla extract** and the shredded carrots.  Let it sit at least 10 minutes, better overnight.

- Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.  Mix in **1 1/2 cups white flour, 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 3/4 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp baking soda, 1/2 cup sugar, 1/4 cup of raisins, handful of shredded coconut, and a handful of crushed walnuts or pecans** with the wet ingredients.  Pour into an 8-inch round pan and smooth down.

- Bake 30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the cake comes out clean.  Let it cool and sit overnight, ideally.

- Set out **2 oz of vegan cream cheese (Tofutti or Miyokos), 1 oz vegan butter (such as Melt or Earth Balance) or coconut butter** to come to room temperature.  Beat the cream cheese and butter with **1/2 cup of powdered sugar** and **1/4 tsp pure vanilla extract**.  Add a little **nondairy milk of choice** as needed.

- Ice the cake with the cream cheese frosting.
