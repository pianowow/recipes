#Roasted Tomato Soup

Makes 4 servings

Preheat the oven to 400 degrees.  Line a half sheet pan with parchment paper.  Stem and cut **3 pounds of roma tomatoes** in half lengthwise.  Peel **8 cloves of garlic** and arrange the tomatoes cut side up and garlic on the parchment paper lined pan.  Spray with a little oil and season with salt and pepper.  Roast in the oven for 40 minutes.


While the tomatoes and garlic are roasting, thinly slice **2 yellow onions**, and add to a large skillet over medium heat.  Add **½ tablespoon of oil** and salt and saute for 20 minutes or until well caramelized.  Stir every few minutes and set aside when they are golden.


Add tomatoes to a large pot and use an immersion blender to blend the tomatoes until smooth.  Then add the onions, garlic, and **½ cup packed basil leaves** and blend again until smooth.  


Add **1 cup of vegetable broth** and **½ cup of coconut milk**, salt and pepper to taste, stir, and simmer for 10 minutes.
