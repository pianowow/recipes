# Ratatouille

I used to slice everything with a mandolin and roast the Disney movie way. But this more traditional ratatouille was so much simpler and ended up being tastier than anything I'd tried that looked like the other way. I used a store-bought Silver Palate San Marzano blend marinara on the last step (blasphemy I know) but it worked.

1. Prep:
   - Dice in half inch cubes **1 Eggplant**, place in a collander, sprinkle with salt and let sit over the sink to drain for at least 20 minutes.
   - Dice in half inch pieces **2 zucchini, 2 yellow squash, 6 roma tomatoes, 1 red bell pepper, 1 yellow onion**
   - Mince **5 cloves of garlic**
2. Once the eggplant dice is drained, sautee with a little oil in a large pan until just fork tender. Reserve in a large bowl.
3. Sautee the zucchini and yellow squash until just tender and reserve with the eggplant.
4. Sautee the onions and peppers until tender, add the garlic and sautee until fragrant, then add the tomatoes. Add herbs and spices: **oregano, thyme, rosemary, crushed red pepper.** Continue to sautee until the tomatoes break down. Salt and pepper to taste.
5. Combine everything reserved into the large pan and heat through. Add prepared marinara if desired and heat everything through.
